    How it works

    The website runs the same steps as the training notebook. Nothing is hard-coded and the model is not retrained per request.

    1. 1Upload or recordThe browser sends the WAV file (uploaded, or recorded with the microphone) to the Flask backend.
    2. 2Load audiolibrosa resamples to the rate used in training (22,050 Hz for the LSTM) and keeps the first second.
    3. 3MFCC features40 MFCCs per frame (n_fft 2048, hop 512), zero-padded to the 44 frames seen in training.
    4. 4ModelThe trained network, loaded once at startup, outputs a probability for each of the 100 classes.
    5. 5ResultThe highest-probability class is mapped back to its number and shown with the top 5 candidates.
